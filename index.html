About Me

Your Local Conservative Councillor

I am proud to serve as the District Councillor for Ling Forest Ward on Mansfield District Council. As a member of the Conservative Party, I believe in empowering local communities, supporting businesses, and ensuring value for money in local services.

Having lived in the area for many years, I understand the unique challenges and opportunities we face in Ling Forest Ward. My background has given me the skills to effectively represent residents and work collaboratively to improve our area.

"I'm committed to listening to residents and fighting for Ling Forest's interests on Mansfield District Council."

My Priorities

Safer Streets

Working with local police to reduce anti-social behavior and improve street lighting in problem areas.

Better Roads

Campaigning for more investment in road repairs and pavement improvements across the ward.

Supporting Local Businesses

Championing our high streets and helping local enterprises recover and thrive.

Cleaner Environment

Tackling litter and fly-tipping while protecting our green spaces.

Community Facilities

Fighting for better maintenance and investment in parks, playgrounds, and community centers.

Value for Money

Ensuring council tax is spent wisely on services that matter most to residents.
